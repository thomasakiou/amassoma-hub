Kẹkẹ́bọ̀lọ̀u

/kɛ̀kɛ́.bɔ̀lɔ̀ʊ̀/
Noun Central Izon • Ogboin Clan Dialect
1.

A riverbank settlement; the traditional landing shore for community canoes and fishermen at dawn.

“Wó kẹkẹ́bọ̀lọ̀u yé dou.”

English: We are heading to the ancestral riverside landing.
