Bọ́lọ̀u

/bɔ́.lɔ̀ʊ̀/

Noun (Watercraft Anatomy) • Directional Adverb

volume_up Standard Bayelsa Citation 0:02
1

Prow, Bow, or Forward Section of a Watercraft

The tapered front extremity of a traditional dugout canoe (arụ) configured to cleave Niger Delta river currents.

“Bọ́lọ̀u mi na gbá tin.”

English: 'Hold the front of the canoe firmly.'

2

Temporal Forwardness; The Days Ahead

Metaphorical extension derived from navigation: facing upstream into the coming season or destination.
