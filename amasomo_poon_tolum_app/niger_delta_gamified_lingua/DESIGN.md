## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a joyful, communal, and intergenerational celebration of indigenous language preservation. Centered on the rich cultural waters and lush flora of Bayelsa, Nigeria, the aesthetic fuses gamified playfulness with cultural pride. The interface is welcoming to both a four-year-old child tapping their first animal sounds and an elder reconnecting with ancestral heritage.

The visual ethos blends **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Micro-Affordances** with **Clean High-Contrast Accessibility**. The UI departs from sterile, flat digital surfaces by introducing physical "chunkiness": heavy, pushable 3D bottoms, bevel-like depth layers, and tactile responsive press states reminiscent of classic physical wooden and plastic learning blocks. The tone is uplifting, encouraging, bright, and vibrant, instilling confidence with zero fear of failure.

## Colors

The palette takes direct inspiration from the vibrant natural ecology of the Niger Delta riverways and Bayelsa mangroves:

- **Hero Jungle Palm Green (`#16A34A` base, `#15803D` bottom/press depth, `#22C55E` accent highlight):** The primary color driving success, progress, level completions, and the primary call-to-action buttons. Evokes vitality, growth, and the abundant lush land.
- **Sun Gold / Topaz Streak Yellow (`#F59E0B` base, `#D97706` depth, `#FBBF24` highlight):** The secondary color dedicated to XP rewards, active day streaks, achievement medals, and currency counts.
- **Kingfisher River Blue (`#0284C7` base, `#0369A1` depth, `#38BDF8` highlight):** The tertiary color reserved for audio/listening exercises, pronunciation wave elements, and grammar information cards.
- **Warm Mangrove Coral (`#EA580C` base, `#C2410C` depth, `#FB923C` highlight):** Used for energy lives/hearts, high-priority notifications, and mistake reminders.
- **Neutral Canvas & Foundations:** Crisp, high-legibility ivory light layers (`#FFFFFF` pure surface, `#F8FAFC` canvas backdrop, `#F1F5F9` neutral stroke/recessed slots) paired against high-contrast slate text (`#0F172A` deep ink text, `#475569` secondary guidance labels).

## Typography

The design system exclusively utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans**, celebrated for its balanced geometric structure, open counters, rounded personality, and exceptional clarity at all scale factors.

- Special diacritical markers and sub-dots common to Izon orthography (such as **ẹ**, **ọ**, and nasal markers) render cleanly with deliberate vertical line spacing to prevent accent clipping.
- **Headlines (800 / ExtraBold):** Punchy, expressive, and encouraging. Conveys achievement milestones, lesson titles, and quiz prompts.
- **Body Text (500 / Medium):** Sturdy, legible, and friendly for both beginner readers and elders requiring higher optical clarity.
- **Interactive Labels (700 & 800 / Bold):** Styled with slight uppercase trackings on badges and button controls to ensure immediate, effortless tap-target identification.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first fluid model optimized for handheld single-thumb operation while easily expanding into a centered maximum reading column (`480px` max constraint) on tablets or desktop screens to preserve native mobile immersion.

- **Column System:** 4 fluid columns on mobile viewports with `16px` (`1rem`) gutters and `20px` (`1.25rem`) edge screen margins. On viewport widths above `768px`, content docks inside a centered card frame with `24px` gutter rhythms.
- **Rhythm & Touch Buffers:** Interactive touch targets strictly adhere to a minimum physical bounding dimension of `48px` (ideally `56px` to `64px` for game nodes and answer pills) with generous vertical spacing (`space-md` to `space-lg`) between questions and option slots to prevent mis-taps.
- **Safe Zones:** Generous dynamic bottom offsets reserved for persistent sticky footer control rails (`84px` height safe area) housing contextual check and proceed actions.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred, hazy shadows. Instead, it creates visual hierarchy and playfulness using **Extruded 3D Chunky Slabs**:

1. **The 3D Tactile Edge:** Buttons, interactive nodes, and selection tiles feature a solid bottom edge border (`4px` for standard elements, `6px` to `8px` for massive game path nodes). The border color is always a saturated 20-30% darker shade of the element's top fill.
2. **Active State Deflation:** When pressed, elements physically translate downwards by `2px` or `4px` (`translateY(3px)`), while the bottom edge collapses, providing instant, satisfying haptic-like visual feedback.
3. **Resting Cards & Surfaces:** Resting informational panels sit on a crisp `2px` border with light neutral undertone (`#E2E8F0`) and a static flat offset shadow (`0 4px 0 #E2E8F0`).
4. **Modals & Bottom Celebration Sheets:** Elevated popups rise above a dark, warm-tinted scrim (`#0F172A` at 60% opacity) with an extruded `4px` solid border, immediately focusing attention during lesson completion or mistake corrections.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, tactile curves with a roundedness level of `2` (`16px` base roundedness, `24px` on interactive cards and modal sheets, full-pill `9999px` on chips, streak indicators, and floating map nodes).

- **Buttons & Answer Tiles:** Feature `16px` (`rounded-lg`) curvature to maximize physical pushability without appearing like harsh digital rectangles.
- **Stepping Stone Nodes:** Pure spherical pills with nested circular icons and concentric ring indicators for completed, current, and locked state stages.
- **Chips & Badges:** Full circular caps (capsule pills) that cradle XP gemstones, streak fire icons, and heart lives securely.

## Components

### 1. Tactile 3D Action Buttons
- **Primary Hero Button:** Lush green background (`#16A34A`), text white (`#FFFFFF`), solid extruded bottom edge of `4px` (`#15803D`). On hover/active, the button moves down `3px`, collapsing the bottom edge to `1px`.
- **Secondary Audio / Replay Button:** Kingfisher river blue (`#0284C7`) with a `#0369A1` base edge, paired with speaker soundwave glyphs.
- **Disabled State:** Solid light gray surface (`#E2E8F0`) with darker gray inset edge (`#CBD5E1`) and dimmed slate copy (`#94A3B8`). No active depression.

### 2. Gamified Stepping Stone Nodes (Level Map)
- Circular buttons (`72px × 72px`) positioned in an organic alternating wave down the path.
- **Current Active Level:** Pulsing outer ring (`#22C55E` at 30% alpha), primary green body with a `6px` bottom bevel, topped with a golden crown or star icon.
- **Locked Level:** Cool neutral surface (`#E2E8F0`), `#CBD5E1` base edge, with a padlocked icon.

### 3. Stat Bar & Resource Chips
- Sticky top app bar holding capsule pills:
  - **Streak Counter:** Sun gold background tint (`#FEF3C7`), gold border (`#FDE68A`), active flame glyph, bold dark text (`#B45309`).
  - **Hearts / Life Counter:** Coral wash (`#FFEDD5`), border (`#FED7AA`), beating heart icon, bold terracotta text (`#C2410C`).
  - **XP Gem Chip:** Deep sky wash (`#E0F2FE`), border (`#BAE6FD`), river kingfisher blue text (`#0369A1`).

### 4. Interactive Quiz Option Cards
- Grid or vertical stack of multi-choice cards (`16px` radius, `2px` stroke border `#E2E8F0`, `4px` bottom depth border `#E2E8F0`).
- **Selected State:** Border and bottom depth change to river blue (`#0284C7`), fill turns into a soft icy blue tint (`#F0F9FF`).
- **Correct Feedback State:** Instantly flashes to vibrant palm green border and background tint (`#F0FDF4`), accompanied by a green badge checkmark.
- **Incorrect Feedback State:** Flashes to mangrove coral border and tint (`#FFF7ED`), revealing the correct pronunciation hint below.

### 5. Sound Wave Listening Trigger
- Large circular button (`56px × 56px`) or pill containing an animated three-bar soundwave glyph. When tapped, it plays the native Izon speaker recording accompanied by an animated scale bounce.

### 6. Bottom Celebration Sheets
- Fixed overlay sheet docking from the screen bottom with `28px` top corner radii.
- **Success:** Emerald green top border, cheerful bouncing mascot/badge, full-width "PỌỌN / CONTINUE" tactile green button, displaying earned XP and streak increments.
- **Correction:** Terracotta/coral accent bar, displaying the exact Izon phonetics, English translation, and an immediate "Got it" action.
